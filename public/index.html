Why I created this system

I created Debt-Free in 90 Days to turn an overwhelming debt problem into a clear, practical 90-day action plan. The goal is not a magic promise — it is to give you a structured way to know your numbers, take action, track progress and keep moving.

No invented credentials. No overnight-money claims. Just a practical system and the tools to work through it.

Debt is a solvable math problem — not a character flaw.

The book deliberately sets a realistic definition of success for 90 days. Depending on your balance and income, “debt-free” in 90 days may or may not be realistic. What matters is measurable reduction, a clear target and a plan you can keep following.
